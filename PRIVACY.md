# Privacy Policy — VaultMind for Jira

Last updated: 27 November 2026

VaultMind for Jira is a Forge app built by M Aziz Yahya, an independent developer. It checks Jira payment and purchase requests against spending-policy rules configured by a Jira admin, such as per-transaction caps, daily caps, vendor and requester allowlists, and a freeze switch.

## Data the app processes

- Jira issue fields needed to evaluate a payment request, such as summary, description, labels, reporter, and project.
- The spending policy configured by the Jira admin on the VaultMind Policy admin page.
- Evaluation results and audit-trail entries stored in Forge app storage for the installing site.

## Data storage and sharing

- Policy settings, verdicts, reservations, and audit entries are stored in Atlassian Forge app storage for the site where the app is installed.
- The app does not sell personal data.
- The app does not send issue data to external servers owned by the developer. Processing happens inside Atlassian Forge.

## Purpose

Data is processed only to evaluate payment requests against the configured policy, show the verdict on the issue, block workflow transitions that violate policy, and keep an audit trail for the site admin.
